# Enterprise Workforce Attendance and Working Hours Policy

## 1. Standard Working Hours and Shifts
- **Standard General Shift**: 09:00 to 18:00 (Monday through Friday), inclusive of a 60-minute lunch and wellness break. Total active daily work requirement is 8.0 hours.
- **Configured Shift Schedules**:
  - General Shift: 09:00 - 18:00
  - Early Morning Shift: 06:00 - 15:00
  - Late Evening Shift: 14:00 - 23:00
- **Grace Period**: Employees have a standard grace period of 15 minutes. Check-ins up to 09:15 are considered on-time. Check-ins after 09:15 are flagged as `LATE` and calculate recorded late minutes.

## 2. Check-In, Check-Out, and Clocking Protocols
- Employees must record their daily presence through the HRMS Attendance portal check-in button upon initiating work and check-out upon concluding work.
- **Duplicate Punch Prevention**: The system strictly prevents duplicate clock-in records on the same calendar day. Attempting multiple check-ins returns a validation error.
- **Half-Day Attendance**: Clocking fewer than 4.0 hours constitutes a half-day absence. Clocking fewer than 2.0 hours is logged as `ABSENT`.

## 3. Missed Punch and Attendance Correction Workflow
- If an employee fails to check in or out due to technical failure, field client engagement, or remote duty, they may submit an Attendance Correction Request via Self-Service within 48 hours of the missed date.
- The request requires:
  - Date of missed punch
  - Requested check-in and check-out timestamps
  - Detailed justification for supervisor review
- **Supervisor Approval**: Once reviewed and approved by the reporting manager, the system automatically overwrites and recalculates the attendance summary, total working hours, and removes the absence or late flag.

## 4. Overtime Guidelines
- Overtime applies to non-exempt roles where cumulative daily hours exceed 8.0 hours or 40.0 hours weekly.
- Overtime must receive pre-authorization from the Department Manager.
- Logged overtime hours are passed to the payroll engine for compensation or compensatory time-off (comp-off) credit.
