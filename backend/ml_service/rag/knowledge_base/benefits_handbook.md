# Enterprise Employee Compensation & Benefits Handbook

## 1. Salary Payment and Payslips
- **Disbursement Schedule**: Salaries are disbursed on the final business day of every calendar month via automated direct bank transfer.
- **Components of Salary**:
  - Basic Monthly Salary (typically 40% - 50% of monthly CTC)
  - House Rent Allowance (HRA)
  - Special & Conveyance Allowances
- **Statutory Deductions**:
  - Employee Provident Fund (PF): 12% of Basic salary
  - Professional Tax (PT): $200.00 standard state levy
  - Tax Deducted at Source (TDS): Based on statutory income brackets and employee investment declarations
- **Payslips**: Payslips are generated within 24 hours of payroll approval and available for secure PDF preview and download in the Self-Service portal.

## 2. Healthcare, Life & Disability Insurance
- **Comprehensive Medical Coverage**: 100% employer-sponsored health, vision, and dental insurance for employees, with 80% coverage for eligible dependents.
- **Group Term Life Insurance**: Coverage equal to 3x annual base salary.
- **Wellness & Fitness Allowance**: $100 monthly reimbursement for gym memberships, mental health counseling, or fitness equipment.

## 3. Learning & Development Allowance
- **Annual L&D Budget**: Every active employee is entitled to up to $3,000 per calendar year for professional certifications, technical conferences, or accredited courses.
- **Tuition Assistance**: Up to $5,000 annually for degree programs aligned with the employee's career progression plan, subject to manager pre-approval.

## 4. Expense Reimbursements
- Eligible business travel, client meals, home-office equipment, and software licenses must be submitted through the Expenses portal within 30 days of purchase.
- Multi-tier approval routing:
  - Up to $500: Department Manager approval.
  - Exceeding $500: Department Manager and Finance Director approval.
- Approved claims are reimbursed in the next immediate monthly payroll run.
