# Enterprise Global Code of Business Conduct & Ethics

## 1. Core Principles
All employees, contractors, and officers are held to the highest standards of integrity, professional respect, confidentiality, and statutory compliance.

## 2. Workplace Harassment & Non-Discrimination
- The enterprise enforces strict zero-tolerance towards harassment, bullying, discrimination, or intimidation based on race, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or national origin.
- Violations result in immediate escalation to HR Compliance and may lead to disciplinary termination.
- Anonymous reports can be lodged via the HR Helpdesk portal under the "Ethics & Compliance" category.

## 3. Confidentiality, Data Protection, and Intellectual Property
- All source code, product specifications, financial structures, customer lists, and proprietary algorithms created during employment are the sole intellectual property of the enterprise.
- Employee data, payroll compensation details, and identity documents are strictly classified and protected under GDPR, CCPA, and enterprise security controls.
- Sharing confidential corporate data on public forums, generative AI services without enterprise agreement, or unauthorized repositories is strictly prohibited.

## 4. Conflict of Interest and Secondary Employment
- Employees must disclose any outside business activities, advisory roles, or financial interests that could conflict with their primary enterprise duties.
- Prior written consent from the HR Operations Manager and Legal counsel is mandatory before undertaking secondary commercial employment.

## 5. Gifts and Hospitality
- Employees may not accept gifts, gratuities, or hospitality exceeding $100 in value from vendors, clients, or partners. Any commercial promotional items must be reported to the Finance Director.
