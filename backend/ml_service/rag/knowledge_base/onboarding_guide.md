# Enterprise Employee Onboarding & Offboarding Lifecycle Guide

## 1. Pre-Boarding & First Day Procedures
- **Pre-Arrival Tasks**: The HR Operations team triggers the onboarding checklist 7 days prior to the employee's start date (`joining_date`).
- **Identity & Compliance**: New hires must upload government identification, tax documentation (Form W-4), and execute the electronic Non-Disclosure & Intellectual Property Agreement.
- **Hardware Issuance**: IT provisions standard enterprise hardware:
  - Engineering: MacBook Pro 16" M3 Max or ThinkPad P1 Gen 6 with 64GB RAM.
  - Other Departments: MacBook Air 15" M3 or Dell Latitude Enterprise.
  - YubiKey 5C security hardware key for multi-factor authentication.

## 2. 30-60-90 Day Milestones & Probation
- **Buddy Program**: Every new hire is paired with an onboarding peer mentor for the initial 60 days.
- **Milestone Check-ins**:
  - 30 Days: Initial check-in with HR Business Partner and Manager regarding team ramp-up and tooling access.
  - 60 Days: Mid-probation review on OKR goal alignment and technical contributions.
  - 90 Days: Formal probation review. Successful completion confirms transition from `Probation` to `Active` permanent employee status.

## 3. Offboarding & Exit Clearance
- **Notice Period**: Standard notice period is 30 calendar days for individual contributors and 60 days for managerial/executive roles.
- **Exit Clearance Checklist**:
  1. Asset Return: Laptops, monitors, security badges, and company credit cards returned to IT inventory.
  2. Access Revocation: Immediate termination of Okta SSO, email, GitHub, and cloud credentials on the last working day.
  3. Knowledge Handover: Documented transition of projects signed off by the Department Manager.
  4. Full & Final (F&F) Settlement: Processed by Payroll within 30 days of exit, computing remaining salary, earned leave encashment, and gratuity if applicable.
